# Code of Conduct

CompanyConnect.Tech expects public technical discussions and contributions to remain respectful, specific, and professional. Treat every contributor, reviewer, prospect, and reader with consideration regardless of role, experience, background, identity, or viewpoint.

Constructive technical disagreement is welcome. Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, disclosure of private information, threats, and attempts to obtain or publish credentials, customer material, or internal systems information are not acceptable.

Report a concern privately to [letsconnect@companyconnect.tech](mailto:letsconnect@companyconnect.tech?subject=Conduct%20concern%20-%20Lead-to-Quote%20Demonstrator). Reports will be handled proportionately and confidentially where possible.
